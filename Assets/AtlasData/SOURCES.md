# Bundled country reference snapshot

Prepared 2026-10-26. The Lens reads bundled TypeScript and PNG assets and makes no network requests.

* Borders: Natural Earth, Admin 0 Countries, 1:110m, public domain. Saved original `natural-earth-countries.geojson` from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson. Natural Earth describes its de facto boundary conventions at https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/disputed-boundaries-policy/ . Geometry is a small-scale educational generalization, not a boundary determination.
* Population: World Bank indicator SP.POP.TOTL, reference year **2024**, downloaded API response saved as `population-2024.json` (database update 2026-10-08). Query: https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/ARG;BOL;BRA;CHL;COL;ECU;GUY;PRY;PER;SUR;URY;VEN/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL?format=json&date=2024&per_page=100 . Figures are estimates and may be revised.
* Capitals, climate and broad religion summaries: public-domain CIA World Factbook profiles preserved by https://github.com/factbook/factbook.json/tree/master/south-america . The complete downloaded profiles are saved in `References/`, named by ISO2. These are archival reference snapshots, not claims of live updates. Religion summaries deliberately omit percentages, do not describe every individual, and include nonreligious people where relevant. Profiles use different survey years (2012–2024); see their `People and Society` / `Religions` fields. Uruguay's archive contains conflicting survey summaries, so no numerical majority is asserted.
* Official-language scope: national-language summaries preserve local recognition. Bolivia: Spanish and 36 Indigenous languages. Ecuador: Spanish nationally, Kichwa and Shuar for intercultural relations, other Indigenous languages in their areas. Colombia: Article 10; Peru: Article 48; Venezuela: Article 9. Constitution references: https://www.constituteproject.org/ . Spanish is labelled **de facto** for Argentina, Chile and Uruguay rather than asserting a federal constitutional designation.
* Currency names and ISO codes: reference identifiers ARS, BOB, BRL, CLP, COP, USD, GYD, PYG, PEN, SRD, UYU and VES; no exchange rates are displayed.
* Flags: https://flagcdn.com/ via Flagpedia, ISO2 PNGs at `https://flagcdn.com/w320/{iso2}.png`, bundled in `Flags/`. Peru uses the civil national flag without the state coat of arms. These are raster flag assets, not emoji glyphs.

The 12 selectable sovereign countries are Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Guyana, Paraguay, Peru, Suriname, Uruguay and Venezuela. French Guiana, when drawn as map context, is explicitly labelled as part of France and is not a thirteenth selectable country. The map retains Natural Earth's outline of Tierra del Fuego with the relevant countries.

`build_country_data.py` reproduces `Assets/Scripts/AtlasCountryData.ts` from the saved borders and population response. Each country entry also records its profile, population and flag URLs.
